# 1 What is Spectroscopy?
- scientific method of studying objects and materials based on detailed patterns of colors (wavelength)
- different materials emit and interact with different wavelengths (colors) of light in different ways, depending on properties like temperature and composition
- can tell us
	- material composition of objects
	- density
	- speed (in space)
		- doppler shift
		- cosmological redshift
	- and more!
# 2 Light and Matter
- Wave-particle nature
	- wavelength and energy essentially the same; difference in color is also difference in energy
- Light interacts with mater in different ways
	- Absorption, emission, transmission, reflection, refraction
# 3 Spectra and Spectroscopy
- Rainbows are spectra that appear naturally when sunlight passes through water droplets, which act like prisms
- All spectra basically show how brightness varies with wavelength
- Continuous, absorption, transmission, emission
	- mine is emission
