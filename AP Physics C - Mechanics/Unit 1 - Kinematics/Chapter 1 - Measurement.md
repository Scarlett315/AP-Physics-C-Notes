## 1.1 Measuring Things, Including Lengths
- measurement by comparison with a standard
- Unit: a unique name we assign to measures of a quantity; the standard corresponds to exactly 1.0 unit of the quantity
- Base Quantity: a small number of physical quantities upon which all other physical quantities are defined (e.g. length, time --> speed)
- SI / metric system base units are length, mass, and time
- Use sig figs!
## 1.2 Time and 1.3 Mass
- Any time standard must be able to answer two questions: "*When* did it happen?" and "What is it's *duration*?"
- SI unit for time is 1 second
- SI unit for mass is 1 kilogram (or 1 amu for atoms)
- Density is mass per unit volume; $\rho=\frac{m}{V}$
