## 3.1 Vectors and Their Components
>[!abstract] Key Ideas
>- Scalars have magnitude only, are specified by a number w/ a unit and obey the rules of arithmetic and ordinary algebra.
>- Vectors such as displacement have both magnitude and direction and obey the rules of vector algebra
>- Vector addition and subtraction geometrically
>- Components & vector resolution, going from magnitude-angle to component form and vice versa

- **Vector**: has magnitude as well as direction; e.g. displacement, velocity, acceleration
	- Identical vectors have the same magnitude and direction; they can be in different places i.e. shifted
- **Scalar**: has a magnitude but no direction; specified a single value with a sign (e.g. -40)
### Vector Addition & Subtraction
- Graphically, the resultant vector after vector addition between two vectors $\vec{a} + \vec{b}$ is the vector from the tail of $\vec{a}$ to the head of $\vec{b}$.
- Properties:
	- Commutative: $\vec{a} + \vec{b} = \vec{b} + \vec{a}$
	- Associative: $(\vec{a} + \vec{b}) + \vec{c} = \vec{a} + (\vec{b} + \vec{c})$
- Vector subtraction between $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$ is defined as $\vec{a} + ^{-}\vec{b}$, where $-\vec{b}$ is defined such that $-\vec{b} + \vec{b} = 0$ (same magnitude and opposite direction)
- Remember to add vectors of the same kind (e.g. adding a velocity to an acceleration vector makes no sense)
### Components of Vectors
- **Vector Component**: the projection of the vector on an axis
- **Vector Resolution**: the process of finding the components of a vector

## 3.2 Unit Vectors, Adding Vectors by Components
### Unit Vectors
- **Unit Vector**: a vector with magnitude of exactly one and points in a particular direction; lacks dimension and unit
	- The unit vectors in the x, y, and z direction are labeled with $\hat{i}, \hat{j}, \hat{k}$ 
	- $\vec{a} = a_x \hat{i} + a_y \hat{j} + a_z \hat{k}$ 
	- $a_x\hat{i}, a_y\hat{j}, a_z\hat{k}$ are the **vector components** while $a_x, a_y, a_z$ are the **scalar components** 
- **Right-Handed Coordinate System**: axes are arranged according to the right-hand rule
